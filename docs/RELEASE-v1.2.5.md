# LingoDesk 1.2.5

- Choose your audio source and languages on the portrait recording screen. The recording controls sit at the bottom, leaving more room for captions.
- Switch between bilingual captions and live notes while recording. Each caption card keeps the original and translation together, with consistent speaker numbering.
- Export a TXT transcript while recording. After stopping, tap Save to keep the recording in history.
- Manage floating captions in Settings. They are enabled by default and appear when Android grants permission to display over other apps.
- Connection errors show more detail when a live session fails to connect.
