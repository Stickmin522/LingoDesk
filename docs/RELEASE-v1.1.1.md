LingoDesk brings live bilingual captions and meeting notes to Android.

- Record microphone audio, system audio, or both.
- Translate between Japanese and Chinese, or English and Chinese, with original text and translation shown together.
- Switch between captions and live notes.
- Read floating captions while using other apps. Move or resize the window; its toolbar hides automatically.
- Keep recording and translating in the background until you stop in the app.
- Play back saved recordings and export audio, captions and notes.

Download `lingodesk-1.1.1-arm64.apk` to install the app. The complete source project is in `lingodesk-1.1.1-source.zip`.
